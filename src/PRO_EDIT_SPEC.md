# KrtLab Professional Video Edit Specification

## Source
- Input: `input/video.mp4`
- Output: `output/final.mp4`
- Format: 1080x1920, 9:16, H.264 + AAC, social-ready.

## Visual direction
- Remove the visible cabinet/background behind the speaker and replace it with a restrained modern office/studio background or clean single-color studio backdrop.
- The replacement must be tracked to the speaker so head/body movement and natural edges do not reveal an artificial background.
- Preserve natural hair/shoulder edges, lighting direction, and realistic depth. Avoid a cut-out/green-screen look.
- Apply subtle skin-safe color correction, contrast, exposure, and sharpening.
- Use restrained punch-in/punch-out zooms on key spoken moments; avoid constant movement.

## Audio
- Clean speech with high-pass/low-pass filtering, gentle compression, and loudness normalization.
- Raise perceived voice level without clipping or audible distortion.
- Optional low-volume background music: instrumental, non-distracting, ducked substantially under speech.

## Captions
- Generate Armenian subtitles from the actual spoken audio.
- Captions must be accurately synchronized and readable on mobile.
- Use short phrase-level lines, strong contrast, and emphasis on important words.
- Do not invent dialogue when transcription is uncertain.

## Motion graphics
- Add minimal professional motion graphics tied to the spoken topic: keyword callouts, simple icons/shapes, subtle transitions, and occasional contextual overlays.
- Graphics should support the speech rather than cover the speaker.
- Use KrtLab visual language: clean, modern, technology/education/productivity feel.

## Quality bar
- The final result should look like a professionally edited founder/product video rather than an AI template.
- No visible editor/tool watermarks or generated branding.
- Keep pacing natural and preserve the speaker's authenticity.
